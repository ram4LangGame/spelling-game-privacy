  <ul>
    <li>Spelling Pops has <strong>no accounts</strong>. You never sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.</li>
    <li>The game <strong>works offline</strong>. Your progress, scores, word lists and settings stay on your phone, and we have no servers of our own.</li>
    <li>The only things that send data anywhere are Google services built into the app: <strong>Google AdMob</strong> (short rewarded videos for free players), <strong>Google Play Billing</strong> (the one-time Pro purchase), <strong>Google Play in-app review</strong> (the rating dialog) and, in builds that include it, <strong>Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics</strong> (a few usage events and crash reports).</li>

<h2 id="who">Who we are</h2>
<p>Spelling Pops is made by ram4LangGame, an independent developer. The source of the game is kept at <a href="https://github.com/ram4LangGame/ShootMissSpellings">github.com/ram4LangGame/ShootMissSpellings</a>. This policy covers the Android app on Google Play. Contact details are at the end.</p>

<h2 id="on-device">What stays on your phone</h2>
<p>Everything the game itself knows about you is stored only in the app's own storage on your device:</p>
<ul>
  <li>your settings (language, sounds, voice, fonts, balloon skin, reminder time, classroom code),</li>
  <li>your word bank, imported word lists and any balloon picture you choose,</li>
  <li>your level progress, placement result, scores, streak and freezes, Daily Ten and weekly challenge records, mock-test papers, drill scores, the words you missed and their revision schedule, the looks you have unlocked and the Pro flag,</li>
  <li>the optional nickname on the local high-score table (up to 24 characters).</li>
</ul>
<p>The app never uploads any of this. <em>Back up scores &amp; progress</em>, <em>Save word bank</em> and <em>Save my game file</em> write a file only to the place you choose (Downloads, or an app you pick in the share sheet). Android's own backup may copy the app's data to your Google account as part of your device backup, under Google's terms, if you have device backup turned on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything the app holds.</p>

<h2 id="admob">Google AdMob rewarded videos (free players only)</h2>
<p>If you have not bought Pro, the game may offer two short videos: <em>Watch to get a heart back</em> when you run out of hearts in a timed mode (once per round) and <em>Watch to reveal a hint</em> (at most twice per level). A video plays only after you tap the offer, nothing is offered when no video is loaded (for example offline), and no part of the game needs a video. There are no banner ads and no pop-up ads.</p>
<p>To show and measure those videos, Google AdMob collects:</p>
<ul>
  <li>your device's <strong>advertising ID</strong> (resettable or deletable in Android Settings → Google → Ads),</li>
  <li>your <strong>approximate location</strong>, derived by Google from your internet address (the app never reads the device's location),</li>
  <li><strong>device and app information</strong>: phone model, Android version, app version, language,</li>
  <li><strong>ad interaction data</strong>: which videos were shown and whether you watched to the end.</li>
</ul>
<p>Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and <em>Settings → Ad privacy options</em> lets you change your answer later. Google's use of this data is described in <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services</a>. Once Pro is bought the app stops loading the advertising SDK and requests no more videos, so this collection stops; the next launch starts without it.</p>

<h2 id="billing">Google Play Billing (the Pro purchase)</h2>
<p>Spelling Pops Pro is a single one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment and keeps the purchase record. The app receives only the purchase state (purchased, pending, cancelled or none) and a purchase token that it uses to confirm the purchase with Google. We never see your name, email address, card number or billing address. <em>Restore purchases</em>, and every return to the app, asks Google Play whether your account owns Pro so it can be unlocked again after a reinstall. Google's handling of purchases is described in the <a href="https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/">Google Play Terms of Service</a> and the <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google Privacy Policy</a>.</p>

<h2 id="review">Google Play in-app review</h2>
<p>After some good results the app may ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. Google decides whether the dialog appears and handles anything you type into it; the app only learns that it asked. Google Play's own policies govern what you submit there.</p>

<h2>Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics</h2>
<p>To learn which parts of the game are used and where it crashes, the app sends these events to Firebase Analytics: <code>level_start</code> and <code>level_complete</code> (level number, stars and question type), <code>mock_complete</code> (source, marks and maximum marks), <code>paywall_view</code> (where the Pro screen was opened from), <code>purchase_success</code> (the product id), <code>ad_reward</code> (which video kind) and <code>review_prompt</code> (why the rating dialog was requested and how many times it has been asked). The events carry no words, no scores from the leaderboard, no nickname and nothing you typed. Firebase Analytics also records its own standard events: first open, session start, screen views, engagement time, app updates and, because the Pro purchase goes through Google Play, the purchase itself with its product id, price and currency.</p>
<p>Firebase adds its own technical details to each event: an app-instance ID that Firebase creates for your installation, the advertising ID where allowed, phone model, Android version, app version, language and the country derived from your internet address. Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes: the stack trace, phone model, Android version, free memory and whether the phone is rooted. Google keeps event data for two months and crash data for ninety days. Details are in <a href="https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy">Privacy and Security in Firebase</a>.</p>
</section>

<h2 id="reminder">Daily reminder (stays on the phone)</h2>
<p>If you turn on <em>Remind me to play every day</em> in Settings, the app shows one local notification a day at the time you chose. It is scheduled on your phone by the app itself; no server, no account and no data are involved. Android 13 and later ask for the notification permission when you turn the reminder on, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.</p>

<h2 id="share">Share card and progress sheet (only where you send them)</h2>
<p><em>Share my result</em> and <em>Share a progress sheet</em> draw a picture on your phone (score or stars, streak, words to practise, the Play Store link) and hand it to the app you choose in the system share sheet. Spelling Pops uploads nothing; what you share, and with whom, is your choice.</p>

<h2 id="speech">Pronunciation</h2>
<p>The <em>Hear</em> button and Dictation use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. Some engines produce the speech on their provider's servers and may send the word to be spoken to that provider under its own policy. Spelling Pops itself sends nothing for this feature.</p>

<h2 id="children">Children</h2>
<p>Spelling Pops is made for exam candidates and secondary-school students aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and that Google holds data about them through the services above, use the Google links in this policy to have it removed, and let us know.</p>

<h2 id="choices">Your choices</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Play free without watching a video.</strong> Every offer has a "No thanks" option and nothing in the game requires one.</li>
  <li><strong>Remove the videos</strong> by buying Pro.</li>
  <li><strong>Change ad consent</strong> in Settings → Ad privacy options (where consent applies), or reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.</li>
  <li><strong>Turn the reminder off</strong> in Settings, or deny the notification permission.</li>
  <li><strong>Delete your data</strong> by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data already held by Google is governed by the Google policies linked in this page.</li>
</ul>
